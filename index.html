GRC Learning GameWorking title · prototype
A hands-on GRC learning game

Learn GRC by doing the job.

Join Northbay Payments, a fictional payments company, as a Junior GRC Analyst. Do the work real analysts do: access reviews, audit reports, findings calls and vendor reviews.

Every job ends with a piece of work you can put in your portfolio.

Start Job 01 How it works

Who it's for

Students

Studying cyber security, IT or business and wondering what GRC work actually looks like day to day.

Career changers

Coming from IT, finance, audit or elsewhere, and need real examples to talk about in interviews.

New starters

Just landed a first GRC role and want safe practice before doing it for real.

Why this is different

Frameworks tell you what good audit work looks like. They don't teach you what to do when the system owner asks you to drop her busiest team, or IT tells you "it's fixed". That judgement is what this game teaches.

Written by a practitioner

Scenarios drawn from real GRC work, not textbook examples.

One connected company

The same people, systems and policies across every job, so it feels like a real workplace.

Judgement, not memorising

Pushback, false alarms, live incidents and difficult conversations, not just quiz questions.

A portfolio piece every time

Findings, reports and action plans you can show an employer.

How it works

Get briefed

Short briefing cards explain the ideas you need, each with a quick check. Skip them if you know it already.

Do the job

Work through real evidence and decisions, with feedback on every choice as you go.

Get reviewed

Aoife, your manager, reviews your work: what to fix, what you did well, and her version where yours differs.

Keep the output

Copy your findings, report or action plan into your portfolio.
